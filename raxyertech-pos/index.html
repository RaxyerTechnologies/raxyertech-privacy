RaxyerTech POS is an internal point-of-sale app. It only processes the data authorized staff need to record sales and send orders to the kitchen. We do not sell data or use it for advertising. You can exercise your rights by writing to raxyercontact@gmail.com.

1. Data controller

Rafael Abonce García, an individual doing business as Raxyer Technologies, located in Salvatierra, Guanajuato, Mexico, is responsible for processing the personal data collected through the RaxyerTech POS app, which it develops and publishes on Google Play.

Privacy contact: raxyercontact@gmail.com.

2. Who this applies to

The app is for internal use by the business that operates it. It is used only by cashiers, kitchen staff and administrators with an account created by an administrator. There is no public sign-up and no data is collected from customers who buy at the establishment.

3. Data we process

  • Account: name, email, role and password (stored encrypted; no one can view it).
  • Usage and security: sign-ins and sign-outs, failed login attempts, device name, IP address, and the date and time of each action (audit log).
  • Operations: recorded orders (products, quantities, amounts, cash received and change) and the user who recorded them.

We do not process sensitive personal data, location, contacts, photos or files on the device.

4. Purposes

  • Authenticate staff and grant the access that matches their role.
  • Record sales and send orders to the kitchen.
  • Keep an audit log for account security and internal control.

All of these purposes are necessary for the app to work. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or commercial purposes.

5. Who we share it with

  • The hosting provider of the server where the data is stored.
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), which delivers app updates.
  • Google LLC, through Google Play, which distributes the app.
  • Competent authorities, when there is a duly grounded legal request.

We do not sell or transfer personal data for commercial purposes.

6. Security

Communications are encrypted with HTTPS, passwords are stored encrypted, access is controlled by role, and the session is kept in the device's secure storage.

7. Retention

Account data is kept while the user has authorized access. Sales and audit records are kept for 5 years for accounting and internal control purposes, and are then deleted.

8. Your rights (ARCO) and account deletion

You can request Access to your data, its Rectification if inaccurate, its Cancellation, or Opposition to its processing, withdraw your consent, or request deletion of your account and data by writing to raxyercontact@gmail.com with:

  • Your name and a way to receive our reply.
  • A document proving your identity.
  • A description of the right you wish to exercise.

We will reply within 20 business days and, if the request is approved, it will take effect within the following 15 business days. Sales records that the law requires us to keep are retained without being linked to your account.

9. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page with their update date.
